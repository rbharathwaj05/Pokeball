# Narrative Bible & Story Guidelines (MM26AI03)

This document establishes the narrative architecture, honesty claim ladder, pacing rules, mathematical metrics, and art direction for the cinematic front end of the MM26AI03 Pokémon Gen I Gauntlet simulator.

---

## 1. Story Structure

A simulation run is presented as an epic, illuminated chronicle organized into five narrative tiers:

| Unit | Scope | Trigger / Data Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Prologue** | "Choosing the Party" | Threat matrix analysis & drafting justification from `artifacts/team.json` |
| **Chapter** (x8) | One Gym Leader | Engine events: `gym_start` -> `gym_clear` / `gym_fail` |
| **Scene** | One Opponent Encounter | Opponent active species changes (entrance until fainted) |
| **Beat** | One Turn (or Collapsed Run) | Enriched `TurnEvent` & extracted `Fact[]` |
| **Epilogue** | Run Summary & Legacy | Statistics, MVP, turning points, and clean sweeps |

---

## 2. The Honesty Claim Ladder (Non-Negotiable)

Every sentence generated by the storytelling engine must be grounded in verified data:

| Level | Definition | Narration Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Engine-Provided** | Reported verbatim by the battle engine log or explicit result struct (e.g. `winner`, `faint`). | Stated plainly as ground truth. |
| **Derived** | Reconstructed mathematically from pre/post observation diffs or static rule tables (e.g. HP delta, type effectiveness, mover speed order). | Stated plainly; tagged with `derived: true` in the Inspector. |
| **Unknown** | Information not captured or exposed by the simulation engine (e.g. opponent moves before being used, opponent AI internal state). | **NEVER narrated.** No guessing, no filler, no speculation. |

### Forbidden Claims ("Never Say" List)
1. **No Invented Lore**: No character personalities, backstories, town lore, or dialogue attributed to gym leaders. Names come only from verified CSV data (`gyms.csv`, `pokemon.csv`).
2. **No Unsimulated Mechanics**: Never claim mechanics that do not exist in the engine (e.g., no Struggle, no 1/256 miss bug, no Bide counter).
3. **No Unbacked Combat Events**:
   - Never say "critical hit" unless `crit === true`.
   - Never say "missed" unless `hit === false`.
   - Never say "super effective" unless `effectiveness > 1.0`.
4. **No Invented Probabilities**: Probabilities like $P(\text{KO})$ or expected damage $\mathbb{E}[D]$ are explicitly labeled with their mathematical terms, never fabricated as "win chances".

## 3. Fact Kinds & Provenance Glossary

| Fact Kind | Provenance | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `GymStart` | Engine | Gym leader encountered with gym name and type. |
| `OpponentEnters` | Engine | Opponent deploys active Pokémon. |
| `MoveUsed` | Engine | Active Pokémon unleashes a named move. |
| `DamageDealt` | Derived | Target loses $N$ HP ($N\%$ of max HP). |
| `Critical` | Engine/Derived | Critical hit modifier ($2\times$ damage) occurred. |
| `Miss` | Engine/Derived | Move failed accuracy roll. |
| `SuperEffective` | Derived | Type multiplier $\ge 2.0\times$. |
| `NotVeryEffective` | Derived | Type multiplier $\le 0.5\times$ (and $> 0.0\times$). |
| `Immune` | Derived | Type multiplier $= 0.0\times$. |
| `StatusApplied` | Engine/Derived | Target afflicted by SLP, PSN, PAR, BRN, FRZ. |
| `FullyParalyzed` | Engine | Pokémon unable to act due to paralysis. |
| `Faint` | Engine | Pokémon HP reached 0. |
| `Switch` | Engine | Tactical switch executed by trainer. |
| `GymClear` | Engine | All gym members defeated. |
| `GymFail` | Engine | All player party members fainted. |

## 4. Pacing Rules

- **Base Speed**: $18\text{ ms}$ per character at $1\times$ speed.
- **Minimum Beat Duration**: $900\text{ ms}$.
- **Dramatic Pauses**:
  - $+600\text{ ms}$ on `Faint` events.
  - $+300\text{ ms}$ on `Critical` hits.
- **Speed Multipliers**: $0.5\times$, $1.0\times$, $2.0\times$, $4.0\times$.
- **Reduced Motion**: Typewriter animations and delays are replaced with instant text rendering when `prefers-reduced-motion` is detected.

---

## 5. Derived Metric Formulas

1. **HP Advantage**:
   $$\text{HP Advantage} = \frac{\sum \text{Player Current HP}}{\sum \text{Player Max HP}} - \frac{\sum \text{Opponent Current HP}}{\sum \text{Opponent Max HP}} \in [-1.0, +1.0]$$
2. **Turning Point**:
   The turn within a gym exhibiting the largest absolute shift $|\Delta \text{HP Advantage}|$ or an inversion of the sign of HP advantage ($\text{sgn}$ flips from negative to positive).
3. **Close Call**:
   A gym won with total player party HP $\le 30\%$, or any party member surviving with $\le 10\%$ HP.
4. **Clean Sweep**:
   A gym completed with $0$ player faints.
5. **MVP (Most Valuable Pokémon)**:
   Party member with the highest number of opponent KOs; ties broken by cumulative damage dealt ($\sum \Delta \text{HP}$).
6. **Beat Importance Score**:
   $$I = 0.40 \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{KO}) + 0.25 \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{Last KO}) + 0.15 \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{Crit KO}) + 0.10 \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{Status}) + 0.10 \cdot \min(1.0, |\Delta \text{HP Adv}|)$$
